## Description (4000 chars max)

Parenting is full of moments you don't know how to handle. The meltdown in the parking lot. The bedtime battle that's gone on 45 minutes. The sibling fight you're too tired to referee.

Kidai is your calm, knowledgeable companion for exactly those moments.

Hold the mic and describe what just happened — in your own words, in your own voice. Add quick context. Get warm, science-backed guidance in seconds.

**GROUNDED IN THE EFRAT METHOD**
Every response is built on EFRAT (Event → Interpretation → Emotion → Response) — a proven framework for understanding what your child is really communicating. Kidai helps you shift from reacting to responding: from "why are they doing this to me?" to "what do they need right now?"

**WHAT YOU GET**
• A clear reinterpretation of your child's behavior — what they're actually trying to achieve
• Specific phrases you can say right now, adapted to your child's age
• What to do and what to avoid in the moment
• A follow-up action for when things are calm
• Every response ends with a grounding reminder to breathe before you act

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Hold the mic and describe the moment in your own words
2. Add quick context: where you are, how your child is feeling, how urgent it is
3. Get warm, personalized guidance — typically in under 15 seconds

**MULTIPLE CHILDREN**
Add profiles for each of your kids. Guidance is always calibrated to their specific age and developmental stage — because what works for a 4-year-old is completely different from what works for a 9-year-old.

**YOUR HISTORY**
Every moment is saved to your device. Review past situations to spot patterns. See how your child's triggers cluster by time, place, and mood.

**DESIGNED FOR REAL MOMENTS**
No long articles. No theory you have to remember. Just the right guidance at the right time, in plain language, the moment you need it.

Everything stays on your device. Your family's moments are never stored online.
